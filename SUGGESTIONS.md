# Feature Suggestions

Ideas for improving net management effectiveness and efficiency.

---

## Scheduling & Coverage

- **Automated sub-finding** — when a regular NCS marks unavailable, automatically email potential subs (filtered by net preference) rather than requiring manual coordinator action
- **Unavailability conflict detection** — warn when a standing assignment NCS has existing unavailability that hasn't been covered yet
- **Advance notice reminders** — email NCS members X days before their session as a heads-up

---

## Coordinator Tools

- **Multi-week bulk assignments** — assign the same NCS to a recurring slot for a date range (e.g., fill in for a traveling member for 3 weeks)
- **Assignment history / audit log** — track who made changes and when; useful when multiple coordinators are active
- **Net statistics dashboard** — sessions run per NCS, fill rate per net, how far in advance slots get filled
- **Printable/exportable schedule** — PDF or CSV export of the weekly calendar for posting to a club website or mailing list

---

## Net Controller Experience

- **Mobile-friendly quick actions** — simplified view optimized for a phone to volunteer or flag a backup from the field
- **Acknowledgment confirmation** — NCS clicks "I'm ready" the day of their session; coordinator sees at a glance who hasn't confirmed
- **Shift trading** — NCS requests a swap with another specific member; both parties confirm; coordinator is notified

---

## Communication

- **Digest emails** — weekly summary to BCs showing all open slots for the coming week
- **Net report submission** — after a session, NCS logs check-in count, propagation notes, etc.; stored per session
- **Announcements** — BC posts a note visible to all NCS members on their dashboard (e.g., "Holiday schedule in effect next week")

---

## Admin / Ops

- **Bulk user import** ⭐ — CSV upload to add a roster of controllers at once instead of one at a time
- **Session archiving** — view and search past sessions without cluttering the active schedule
- **Multiple clubs / net groups** — tenant-level separation if the app is ever shared beyond OMISS
