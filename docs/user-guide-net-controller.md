# Net Controller User Guide

This guide covers everything a Net Controller (NCS) needs to manage their schedule in NCS Scheduler.

## Your Dashboard

After logging in, **Schedule → Dashboard** is your home base. It shows:

- Your upcoming assigned sessions
- Open slots in nets you cover (sessions with no one assigned)
- A link to your personal iCal calendar feed

---

## iCal Calendar Feed

Subscribe to your personal calendar feed to see your NCS assignments in any calendar app (Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Outlook, etc.).

Your feed URL is shown on your dashboard. Copy it and add it as a **subscribed calendar** (not an import — subscribe so it stays current).

> The URL contains a private token. Do not share it. If it's ever compromised, contact a coordinator or SuperAdmin to regenerate it.

---

## Reporting Unavailability

If you can't run net control for a period of time, report it so the Band Coordinator knows to find a sub.

1. Go to **Unavailability → Report Unavailability**.
2. Enter your start and end dates.
3. Choose whether this applies to **all nets** or a specific net.
4. Save — the coordinator will be able to see your unavailability when managing assignments.

You can view and delete your existing unavailability entries from the same page.

---

## Volunteering for Open Slots

If you're available and see an open slot on your dashboard, you can volunteer:

1. Click **Volunteer** next to the open session.
2. Your callsign will appear on the slot as a volunteer (shown in italics to the coordinator).
3. The Band Coordinator may confirm you or assign someone else — either way, you'll see the result on your dashboard.

---

## Backup Requests

If you have an assigned session but would like a backup standing by in case something comes up:

1. Click **Request Backup** on your upcoming session.
2. NCS members who have opted into backup alerts will receive an email and can offer to stand by.
3. Up to 2 backups can stand by for any session.
4. The ⚠ badge on your session shows how many people are standing by.

To cancel a backup request, click **Cancel Backup** on the session.

---

## Net Preferences

Tell the system which nets you're willing to cover. This helps Band Coordinators find available substitutes.

1. Go to **Volunteer → Net Preferences** (or the preferences link on your dashboard).
2. Check the nets you're willing to cover.
3. Save.

Your preferences are visible to Band Coordinators when they look at the Controllers list. You can update them any time.

---

## Password Reset

If you forget your password:

1. Go to the login page and click **Forgot your password?**
2. Enter your email address.
3. Check your email for a reset link (valid for a limited time).
4. Set a new password using the link.

If you don't receive the email, check your spam folder. If it still doesn't arrive, contact a SuperAdmin.

---

## Account / Profile

Update your display name, callsign, or email address from your **Profile** page (click your name in the top-right corner after logging in). Email changes take effect immediately and are used for all future notifications.
