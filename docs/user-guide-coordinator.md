# Band Coordinator User Guide

Band Coordinators manage NCS assignments for their nets. This guide covers day-to-day tasks.

## Your View

You can only see nets that are assigned to you. If a net is missing, ask a SuperAdmin to assign you as coordinator for that net.

---

## Assignments Page

**Assignments** is your main working view. It shows every upcoming session for your nets in date order, with the current assignment for each slot.

### Assignment status at a glance

| What you see | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Callsign (normal) | Regular or confirmed assignment |
| *Callsign (italic)* | Volunteer — signed up but not yet confirmed |
| **Open** | No one assigned — needs attention |
| ⚠ Backup badge | Session has standby volunteers listed |

Hover over any callsign to see that person's standing net assignments and the date they last ran net control.

### Assigning a substitute

1. Click the assignment cell for the session you want to change.
2. Select a net controller from the list or type to search.
3. Choose the assignment type (Substitute, Volunteer, etc.) and save.

The assigned NCS will receive an email notification if email is configured.

### Manually opening a slot

If an assignment needs to be cleared so someone can volunteer:

1. Click the assignment cell.
2. Choose **Open** (removes the current assignment).

### Assigning outside the normal schedule

Use **Assign Sub for Any Date** to create a session on a date that doesn't appear in the normal schedule (e.g., a special net, a holiday makeup session). This creates a one-off session without modifying the schedule rules.

---

## Weekly Calendar

**Assignments → Calendar** shows the full week (Sunday–Saturday) in a format designed for copy-pasting into an email or message.

- Changed assignments since the previous week are **highlighted** so you can see at a glance what's different.
- Use the week navigation arrows to move forward or backward.

---

## iCal Feed

You can subscribe to a calendar feed that shows all sessions with the assigned NCS. The feed URL is shown at the top of the Assignments page.

Copy that URL and add it as a subscribed calendar in Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Outlook. The calendar updates automatically as assignments change — you don't need to re-subscribe.

> The feed URL contains a private token. Do not share it publicly. If the token is compromised, contact a SuperAdmin to regenerate it.

---

## Net Controllers List

**Controllers** (read-only for Band Coordinators) shows all active net controllers with their contact info and the nets they are willing to cover. Use this when looking for a substitute.

---

## Common Tasks

### Someone can't run their slot — find a sub

1. Check the **Controllers** list for someone covering that net who isn't already assigned that day.
2. Reach out to confirm availability.
3. Update the assignment on the **Assignments** page.

### A volunteer signed up — confirm or replace them

Volunteer assignments show in italics. If you want to confirm the volunteer, leave the assignment as-is — it will run. If you prefer someone else, click the cell and reassign.

### Backup standby is showing — what does that mean?

The ⚠ badge means one or more net controllers have offered to stand by as backup for that session. Their callsigns are visible in the tooltip. This is informational — the primary assignment still stands unless you change it.

### A net isn't showing any sessions

Sessions are generated 9 weeks ahead. If sessions are missing, check with a SuperAdmin that the net is **Active** and has schedule rules configured.
